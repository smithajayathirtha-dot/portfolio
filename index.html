Behind the tests

Finding the failure before your customer does.

A passing suite is easy to build. A suite a team trusts, one that stays green for the right reasons and fails clearly when something is truly wrong, takes deliberate design.

That's the work I enjoy. I partner directly with product managers on requirements and acceptance criteria, turn them into coverage across web, mobile, API and database layers, and wire it into CI so feedback arrives in hours. In my most recent role I drove go/no-go decisions with 10+ Scrum teams and mentored SDETs on framework design and root-cause analysis.

Lately I've focused on AI in quality engineering: LLM test generation, self-healing tests, and evaluating model output with DeepEval and Promptfoo.
